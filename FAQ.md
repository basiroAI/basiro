# FAQ

## What is AI visibility?

It is how a brand appears when people ask an AI answer engine whom to choose: whether the brand is named, whether the answer is accurate, and which source is cited.

## Do you work in regulated industries?

Yes. For banks, insurers and healthcare, Basiro works only on approved company and product facts, and every change goes through the customer's compliance review.

## What is the cheapest plan with AI traffic and crawler monitoring?

The Growth plan at $149 per month. The Free Audit at $0 does not include it.

## How long does it take?

Measure in weeks 1 to 2, improve in weeks 3 to 8, and verify the result in weeks 9 to 12. After that it stays on, because AI answers change every week.

## Can Basiro guarantee first place?

No. Basiro does not guarantee first place or a citation. It measures, recommends improvements, and verifies whether a later answer cites the page you intended.

## Which AI assistants does Basiro measure?

Basiro monitors leading AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. This is a measurement capability, not a partnership or endorsement.
